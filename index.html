How to use the numbers.

1

Compare your options by Location Index.

Choosing between several addresses? The higher index is the stronger location.

2

When two are close, look underneath.

If the likely ranges overlap, treat the locations as tied. Then look at which component matters more to your plan: Staying Power if you expect a slow build, Customer Draw if your plan depends on volume.

3

Watch for the warning flag.

If Customer Draw or Staying Power is unusually low, Tupll flags it, even when the headline number looks fine.

Tupll compares locations, not businesses.

It answers one question: if the same owner opened the same shop in each of these places, which place compares better?

It cannot see the things that are up to you or specific to the site. A great operator in an average location can outperform a weak operator in a great one. Use the numbers to choose between locations and to spot problems early, alongside your own judgment and a visit to the site.

Questions.

Who is this for?

An independent owner considering one or more Texas addresses for a coffee shop, including a first-time owner deciding whether to sign a lease. More business types will follow.

What will I receive, and when?

A web report for your address with the Location Index, Customer Draw, and Staying Power, and the neighborhood detail behind them. It arrives within three business days of payment. Your delivery date appears before payment and is repeated in a confirmation email.

Can it tell me whether my shop will succeed?

No. Tupll compares locations, not businesses. The numbers are relative: they show how this location compares with the locations of existing independent coffee shops. They say nothing about sales, and they cannot see your product, staff, rent, building, parking, or how easy it is to pull into the lot.

What does 100 mean?

Typical for the locations of existing independent coffee shops. It is not a percentage and not a grade. Above 100, the location compares better than typical on what Tupll measures; below 100, it compares worse.

I am comparing several addresses. How does that work?

Order a report for each address. With several saved locations you see them side by side in one table and sort by what matters most to you: the balanced Location Index, Customer Draw, or Staying Power.
