Who is this for?

An independent owner considering one Texas address for a coffee shop, including a first-time owner deciding whether to sign a lease.

What will I receive?

A web report covering the trade area, residents, daytime workers, local change, coffee competition, an area-conditions index, and five best-customer neighborhoods. The $299 option also includes a USGS aerial image and a 15-minute call.

When will I receive it?

Within three business days of payment, with a target of two. Your delivery date appears before payment. A confirmation email within one minute names your preparer and repeats that date.

Can it tell me whether the shop will succeed?

No. The index summarizes public-data conditions, not sales or business success. Rent, operating costs, visibility, parking, and the experience at the storefront still need your judgment.

What is the refund policy?

Read the refund policy before paying. You will also review a summary and accept the terms at checkout.
