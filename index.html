Questions.

Who is this for?

An independent owner considering one Texas address for a coffee shop, including a first-time owner deciding whether to sign a lease.

What will I receive, and when?

A web report covering the trade area, residents, daytime workers, local change, coffee competition, an area-conditions index, and five neighborhoods that fit a coffee-shop profile. It arrives within three business days of payment, with a target of two. Your delivery date appears before payment and is repeated in a confirmation email.

Can it tell me whether the shop will succeed?

No. The index summarizes public-data conditions, not sales or business success. Rent, operating costs, visibility, parking, and the experience at the storefront still need your judgment, and a site visit.
