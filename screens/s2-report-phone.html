What to check on your visit.

Visit the two nearest independents at 8 am on a weekday and count the line.

Check whether the nearest chain has a drive-through.

Note which shops have seating for laptops and how full it is at 10 am.

Ask the landlord for the last two tenants at this address and why they left.

Walk the entry from the arterial: is the turn-in signalized and is parking visible from the road.

4. Sources describe different periods. Each section states its data vintage.
