How the index is calculated

Index = 0.35 × Demand fit + 0.25 × Competitive room + 0.20 × Access + 0.20 × Momentum, rounded to the nearest whole number. Each sub-score is the average percentile rank of its components among existing Texas coffee-shop locations, computed with the same trade-area rule. Components with no data for this address are dropped and named; if a whole sub-score is unavailable, the index is not computed. Full rules: methodology version 1.0.

What to check on your visit.

  • Visit the two nearest independents at 8 am on a weekday and count the line.
  • Check whether the nearest chain has a drive-through.
  • Note which shops have seating for laptops and how full it is at 10 am.
  • Ask the landlord for the last two tenants at this address and why they left.
  • Walk the entry from the arterial: is the turn-in signalized and is parking visible from the road.

Source: Overture Places, August 2026 release. Texas sales-tax locations for first-sale dates.

What this report cannot tell you.

  1. The site index describes public-data conditions. It is not a forecast of sales or success.
  2. The customer-neighborhood profile is rule-based, not learned from your customers.
  3. First-sale dates come from Texas records and are provided only for Texas locations where available.
  4. Sources describe different periods. Each section states its data vintage.
  5. A site visit must check visibility, entry, parking, pedestrian activity, competitor use, and the premises themselves.
