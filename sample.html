Strong. This location scores higher than 85 percent of existing independent coffee shop locations in Texas.

A 100 is a typical location. 182 means the model expects this corner to draw roughly 80 percent more customer traction than a typical existing independent coffee shop site.

The number is built from what is around the address: who lives nearby, who works nearby by day, what else is open on the surrounding blocks, how the streets and buildings are arranged, and what the local coffee market already looks like. The twelve statistics that follow are the ones that matter most and that need no explanation.

Tupll compares locations, not businesses. The number is relative and says nothing about sales. It cannot see your product, staff, prices, rent, the building, parking, signage, or how easy it is to pull in and out of the lot. A strong operator in an average location routinely beats a weak operator in a strong one.
